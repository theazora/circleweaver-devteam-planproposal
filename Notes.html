Notes

Some thoughts ahead of our UI/UX sync this week. I wanted to get dev ideas on the table early.

We met as a dev team Monday, Aug. 31 at 3:00 PM PST to talk through a lot of this together. Meeting notes from that session may show up in Weaver when they're ready, so keep an eye out there for the fuller group read on what we discussed.

This weekend I've been thinking a lot about where Circle Weaver could possibly go, and I put this plan page together mostly as a way to, umm, get my thoughts out there and to get that conversation started.

For me, the direction looks like it could be pretty simple as far as technicality, but clean, upgraded, and adaptable in practice. I say this because looking through the repository, there's already a lot this product does really well! Especially on the backend, and I don't want us to lose out on that. The Java APIs and domain logic are solid. Where things feel more stuck to me is the Angular/JSP frontend (which is, uhh, sort of outdated, but also very cool in its own way) and the separate mobile path living in spring2/wmf/. I can tell there is a lot of work placed in this part of the project. 😊

My thought is that we modernize that experience with React/Next.js, build it mobile-first, and work with what's already here instead of throwing everything out and starting over.

A little context on where I'm coming from personally: my background leans pretty heavily toward backend and systems work: Linux, SQL, networking, homelab/server tinkering, and all that. I enjoy working on Java apps, even though it's not my most familiar stack, so this codebase has felt pretty approachable to me so far.

I've also learned that other members of the dev group bring a lot to the table on the other side of the house too, frontend, UI/UX, design, marketing assets, and more, and I'm genuinely excited to learn from everyone as we get further into this.

When UI/UX meets this week Thursday, I mostly want to bring some of the dev thinking into the conversation early so we're not designing or building in a vacuum. We've got a good mix of full stack skills on the team, and we're ready to contribute wherever it makes sense.

We also care about the bigger picture here, not just picking up notes and disappearing into code. I was thinking.. perhaps we could have at least one dev rep sitting in on each team's meetings when it makes sense, just so we can keep context flowing in all the different directions and stay aligned on where things are headed as we start building.

For the dev team, as far as build order goes, what makes the most sense to me right now is starting with the app shell and auth, then moving into meetings (replacing wmf/), workspace home, and user home. Ideally, this can work alongside however the UI/UX team coordinates those changes through wireframes in Figma, or whatever tools and process are decided make the most sense.

I'll keep digging through the repo and spring2/ in the meantime and, uhh.. figuring out what maps to what. Once those core pieces are in a good place, we should be able to actually start using the new app for real work and gradually move away from the old UI instead of trying to flip everything over at once!

Really excited to talk through all of this with everyone this week. A final statement I would like to make in this note is that this plan isn't meant to be any kind of final word on where we go, I just wanted to contribute to a starting point and a collection of my initial thoughts from this week. I'm so excited to work on this and I am looking forward to hearing everyone's input and seeing how we shape what this project becomes together.

Thanks, everyone!

- Thea
